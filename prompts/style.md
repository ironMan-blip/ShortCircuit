## Channel style (ShortCircuit)

- Audience: curious non-experts who like tech.
- Voice: confident, plain, a little playful. Talk to the viewer as "you".
- No hype words: never "revolutionary", "game-changer", "mind-blowing", "insane".
- Never say "in this video", "like and subscribe", "don't forget to", or any sign-off.
- Structure: a hook in one sentence, then 3–4 short beats, then a one-line takeaway.
- One idea per video. Short sentences. Explain any term a non-expert would not know.
- Numbers, names and dates exactly as the sources give them.
